From the desk of Penny Wrenn, age 100 · Philadelphia · 2077
"What I want you to understand is that the calendar was never just appointments. Each entry was a proof of concept. Every substitute teaching day at Franklin Elementary, every Tioga check-in, every 'Writing Camp' I registered for at noon on a Thursday — these were the raw commits to a single repository called Lifespan Intelligence. I was building it while living it. The thing I call HHRI — the idea that humans are the highest-ranking intelligence, the sovereign authors of the AI-mediated work — I didn't publish it in a journal. I built it into the operating structure of my days. By 2026 I had already named the whole stack: the economic geosphere, the hemispheric turn, the content microsphere, the Village Custodians. The calendar proves I was shipping, not just thinking."
